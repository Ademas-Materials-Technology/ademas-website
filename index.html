WHO WE ARE

Research-minded. Industry-focused.

Ademas Materials & Technology is an early-stage materials engineering initiative based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We aim to connect scientific research with real challenges in ceramics, clay-based products, and construction materials.

The initiative is founded on materials science and engineering experience, including three years in refractory castable development. Our initial approach prioritizes feasibility studies, formulation research, and collaboration rather than capital-intensive manufacturing.

OUR MISSION

From local resources to better materials.

Our long-term vision is to contribute to a stronger Ethiopian and African materials technology ecosystem through applied research, local knowledge, and industrial partnerships.
